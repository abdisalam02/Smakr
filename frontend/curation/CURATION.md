# SMAKR — Oslo & Lillestrøm Food Curation Standard (`CURATION.md`)

## 1. Brand Identity & Aesthetic Fit
Smakr is a mobile-first food discovery radar for **Oslo and Lillestrøm**, built around a playful, hand-drawn sketchbook aesthetic (gouache/marker dish illustrations, Open Peeps character busts, Warm Oat paper `#FAF7F2`, and Burnt Paprika `#E84A27` accents).

- **Culinary Lane:** High-craft casual eats, viral snacks, cult bakeries, specialty coffee/matcha/boba, limited pop-ups, and soulful regional comfort meals.
- **Core Price Sweet Spot:** `45 kr – 260 kr` per individual snack, pastry, drink, or counter meal.
- **Shareable Platter Allowance:** Up to `~350 kr` strictly for shareable casual platters (e.g., whole Neapolitan pizzas, K-fried chicken boxes, or mandi rice platters).
- **Editorial Rule on "Smakr Pick":** **Agents NEVER assign, toggle, or overwrite `Smakr Pick`.** The weekly `Smakr Pick` crown is chosen exclusively by the founder/admin. Agents may only note high-momentum spots in a separate `founder_review_note` field.

---

## 2. Portfolio Balance: The 70 / 30 Split
Every scouting batch must maintain an approximate **70% Trending / 30% Under-the-Radar** balance:

1. **70% — Viral, Trending & New Drops:**
   - Spots generating real momentum on Oslo food TikTok/Instagram, newly launched concepts, or limited weekend pop-up drops.
2. **30% — Under-the-Radar Neighborhood Standouts:**
   - Owner-run spots with **4.6★ – 4.9★** and **25 – 300 Google reviews** where locals repeatedly praise **one unmistakable signature dish**, even without a big social media marketing budget.

---

## 3. The 5 Approved Spot Archetypes
Every candidate spot must fit into at least one of these five categories:

1. **Cult Bakeries & Pastry Drops:**
   - Laminated viennoiserie, cardamom/cinnamon buns (`kanelbolle`), artisan sourdough, small-batch cookies, and seasonal drops.
   - *Benchmarks:* Kveitemjøl, Håndbakt, Crantz Cookies, Sukkerbakeriet.
2. **Obsessive Counter-Service & Street-Food Specialists:**
   - Focused menus built around 1–3 hero crafts: Korean fried chicken, wood-fired Neapolitan slices, crispy bánh mì, smash burgers with real culinary identity, birria tacos, or hand-pulled noodles.
   - *Benchmarks:* Em Oi!, Digg Pizza, 175°C, U Wan Sum.
3. **Specialty Coffee, Ceremonial Matcha, Boba & Gelato Bars:**
   - Third-wave espresso bars, ceremonial matcha counters, artisan boba/tea bars, and small-batch gelato or soft-serve spots.
   - *Benchmarks:* Kuro, Ochaya.
4. **Pop-Ups, Food Trucks & Indie Market Stalls:**
   - Temporary kitchen takeovers, weekend-only bake drops, mobile food trucks, and standout stalls at *Vippa*, *Oslo Street Food*, or *Mathallen*.
   - *Benchmarks:* Mintonwheels, U Wan Sum.
5. **Soulful Regional Comfort Meals (Scratch-Made):**
   - Generous, deeply authentic comfort plates and bowls made from scratch: spiced mandi rice platters, slow-cooked curries/thali, handmade dumplings/bao, or charcoal skewers with house-baked flatbread.
   - *Benchmarks:* Mandi House, Yum Cha.

---

## 4. Hard Exclusions (Instant Rejects)
Agents must **NEVER** include any of the following:
- **Fine Dining & White-Tablecloth Restaurants:** Tasting-menu temples, formal Michelin fine dining, or stiff multi-course restaurants (e.g., Maaemo, Kontrast, Statholdergaarden).
- **Alcohol-Centric Bars, Pubs & Nightlife:** Wine bars, cocktail lounges, sports pubs, or spots where alcohol is the primary draw. *(The `drinks_bars` category in Smakr is reserved for specialty coffee, matcha, boba, fresh juices, mocktail/dessert bars, and snack counters).*
- **Generic Kebab & Late-Night Gatekjøkken:** Standard corner kebab/rullekebab joints, generic pizza-kebab-burger takeaways, or low-craft fast food. *(Middle Eastern/Levantine spots are ONLY allowed if they specialize in authentic charcoal skewers, fresh-baked flatbreads/manakish, or slow-cooked mandi with strong local acclaim).*
- **Multinational & Mass-Market Chains:** McDonald's, Burger King, Peppes Pizza, Egon, Olivia, Espresso House, Joe & The Juice, Waynes, etc.
- **Generic Tourist Traps:** Laminated photo-menu restaurants along Karl Johans gate with no local foodie following.

---

## 5. Geographic Coverage (Inner Oslo, Outer Oslo & Lillestrøm)
Scout across all three concentric zones:
1. **Inner Oslo:** `Grünerløkka`, `Sentrum / Kvadraturen`, `Torggata / Youngstorget`, `Frogner / Vika`, `Bjørvika`, `Majorstuen`, `St. Hanshaugen`, `Vulkan / Mathallen`.
2. **Outer & East/North Oslo:** `Tøyen / Grønland`, `Sagene / Torshov`, `Løren / Hasle`, `Ensjø / Kampen`, `Furuset`, `Stovner`, `Mortensrud`, `Lambertseter`.
3. **Greater Oslo Corridor:** `Lillestrøm` (especially independent bakeries, smash burger/street-food counters, and specialty coffee/matcha spots near Lillestrøm stasjon / Torvgata).

---

## 6. Dietary & Inclusive Scouting Rules
- **Active Dietary Coverage:** Intentionally include verified **Halal** spots alongside strong **Vegan**, **Vegetarian**, and **Gluten-Free** spots.
- **Zero Guessing:** Only attach a dietary tag (`halal`, `vegan`, `vegetarian`, `gluten_free`, `spicy`) when explicitly confirmed on the venue's menu, Wolt/Foodora listing, official Instagram bio, or verified local reviews.

---

## 7. Multi-Channel Scouting & Anti-Hallucination Protocol

### Approved Discovery Sources
1. **Oslo Food Creator Roster:** Track public mentions and videos from `@matmeg_oslo`, `@youngjolasie`, `@eatbyle`, and local bakery/coffee creators. *(Flag any spot covered by 2+ creators within 60 days).*
2. **Meta Ad Library (Norway):** Search active Instagram/Facebook ads in Norway with keywords like `"Oslo"`, `"Lillestrøm"`, `"pop-up"`, `"drop"`, `"matcha"`, `"smash"`, `"birria"`, `"kanelbolle"`, `"halal"` to catch brand-new launches early.
3. **Wolt & Foodora Spotlights:** Scan "New on Wolt" and high-rated neighborhood carousels in Oslo and Lillestrøm (filtering out all chains).
4. **Local Cultural Press & Forums:** *Vink* (Aftenposten), *Subjekt*, *NATT&DAG* opening columns, and `r/oslo` food recommendation threads.
5. **Google Maps Under-the-Radar Sweep:** Filter for `4.6★ – 4.9★` with `25 – 300 reviews` in Inner/Outer Oslo and Lillestrøm.

### Two-Step Verification Gate (Mandatory)
Every spot MUST pass verification before being returned:
1. **Confirm Existence & Exact Pin:** Verify exact street address, neighborhood, and `lat/lng` coordinates via Google Maps / OpenStreetMap.
2. **Verify Real Menu & Price:** Confirm the exact Norwegian/English menu name and current NOK price via Wolt, Foodora, or the venue's official menu. **If a dish name, price, or open status cannot be verified, discard the spot.**

## 8. Icon Mapping & Copywriting Standards
- **One Hero Dish Per Spot:** Pick the single most iconic dish from the venue and map it to an existing key in `DISH_ICON_WEBP` / `FOOD_ICONS_CATALOG` (e.g., `k_chicken`, `pizza`, `pasta`, `ramen`, `banh_mi`, `croissant`, `cinnamon_roll`, `cardamom_bun`, `mandi_rice`, `curry`, `kebab`, `burger`, `tacos`, `sushi`, `dumplings`, `bao`, `matcha`, `coffee`, `gelato`).
- **Missing Icon Flag:** If a standout dish has no matching icon yet, set `icon_status: "NEW_ICON_NEEDED"` and describe the exact single-dish visual for illustration—never overwrite or alias existing icons.
- **Copywriting Tone:** Write crisp, concrete, appetizing descriptions (1–2 sentences max) naming specific textures, ingredients, or preparation methods. **Strictly ban AI clichés** (*"hidden gem"*, *"culinary journey"*, *"burst of flavors"*, *"underrated"*, *"elevated"*).
